Under Pressure

A Study on the Impact of Self-Imposed Stress on Student Interpersonal Relationships

Introduction

  • Stress is a multifaceted response affecting many individuals globally.
  • It impairs decision-making and emotion regulation, leading to increased irritability.
  • Stress alters social behaviour, reducing empathy, trust, and cooperation.
  • People often turn to digital or family support networks to cope.
  • Family dynamics can buffer or intensify stress responses.
  • The COVID-19 pandemic highlighted the importance of social connectedness in resilience.

Theme One

Stress and Its Effects on Social Behaviour

  • Increased cortisol levels lead to less emotional availability.
  • High stress correlates with higher levels of conflict in friendships.
  • Social withdrawal is a common stress coping mechanism.
  • Negative social interactions reinforce anxiety and insecurity.

Theme Two

The Family’s Role in Stress Regulation

  • Secure family relationships act as a stress buffer.
  • Authoritative parenting is linked to lower adolescent stress.
  • Supportive communication helps regulate emotions.
  • Stress contagion within families can either support or worsen coping mechanisms.
